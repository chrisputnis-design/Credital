How it works

Four steps from deposit to interest.

  1. 1

    Step 1: Open an account

    Verify your identity. Every lender and borrower is identity-checked before any loan is made.

  2. 2

    Step 2: Choose your loans

    Each loan is credit-rated from AAA to CCC and shows its interest rate and term. Pick loans yourself, or set your rules and let the platform invest for you.

  3. 3

    Step 3: Borrowers repay

    Borrowers repay in monthly instalments. You can spread your money across many loans instead of relying on one borrower.

  4. 4

    Step 4: You earn interest

    Interest and principal reach your account every month. Reinvest it, or withdraw what has been repaid.

AAACCC

How the rate is set. Two things decide the interest rate on a loan: the borrower's credit rating and the length of the loan. Higher risk or a longer loan pays more. Every rate is shown as an annual percentage, so you can compare it with other investments.

Lending carries risk.

Borrowers can miss payments, and you can lose some or all of the money you lend. Credital is not a bank, and money lent through it is not a deposit.
